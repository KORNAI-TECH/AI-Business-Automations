## 🛠 Featured Solutions

### 1. [🤖 AI Meeting Secretary](./ai-meeting-secretary)
**Problem:** Hours lost on manual meeting minutes and lost context after calls.
**Solution:** An automated Telegram-based agent that captures 100% of the conversation, identifies participants, and generates professional `.docx` reports.
*   **Key Tech:** OpenAI Whisper V3, Llama 3.3 70B, Gemini 1.5 Flash.
*   **Business Impact:** Saves ~45 minutes of manual work per 1 hour of meeting.
